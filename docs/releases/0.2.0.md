## EchoFetch 0.2.0

- **EchoFetch now updates itself.** It looks for a new version when it starts and every hour after that. When one is ready, it installs it and reopens by itself, once nothing is downloading or setting up its tools. Your save folder, history and settings stay as they are.
- **Check for Updates…** is in EchoFetch's app menu, and **Settings → Updates** turns automatic checks and automatic installs on or off and shows when EchoFetch last checked.
- Every update is signed, and EchoFetch refuses anything that isn't signed by its own key.
- EchoFetch now goes online to check GitHub for a new version of itself, as well as for its download engine. It still has no accounts and no tracking.

Install this version by hand once (download `EchoFetch.zip`, replace the old app in Applications). After that, new versions arrive on their own.
